## Showrunner plan

This consolidated 8-step roadmap directly addresses critical quality bottlenecks—eliminating stray TTS stage directions (1.0/5.0 audio defect), synchronizing hook copy 1:1 with audio, implementing mobile safe-zone clamping, and replacing intimidating algebraic notation with high-engagement binary choices ('Move A vs Move B') and tactile domestic micro-stakes under 50 seconds.

1. **Strict Schema Sanitization to Eliminate Stray Spoken Stage Directions** (story, effort low)
   - Isolate spoken narration into a strictly validated `voiceover_text` field in the script schema. Strip inline parentheticals, tone cues, and persona prefixes ('Warm, friendly storyteller:'), passing vocal instructions strictly via Gemini TTS API configuration parameters.
   - Effect: Instantly eliminates stray stage directions being spoken aloud, driving the critical audio quality and voice matching scores from 1.0 to 8.0+.
2. **Strict 1:1 Audio-Visual Hook Synchronization and Safe Positioning** (hook, story, effort low)
   - Enforce a pipeline validation gate where the first spoken voiceover sentence (0:00-0:02) and the on-screen hook overlay share the identical core 4 to 6 words verbatim. Render this text in Inter Bold champagne gold over a translucent night-blue scrim pill restricted to y=280–480px.
   - Effect: Eliminates cognitive dissonance between text and voice at 0:00, drastically curbing first-second feed drop-off and visual occlusion of hero props.
3. **Standardize Binary 'Move A vs Move B' Dilemma & DM Keyword CTA** (hook, story, growth, effort low)
   - Replace open-ended multi-move calculations and algebraic notation asks with a unified binary choice ('Option A or Option B?'). Synchronize on-screen arrows, voiceover CTA, and caption Line 1 with a keyword DM hook: 'Which move saves Vektor: A or B? Comment A, B, or MATE below for the engine answer 👇'.
   - Effect: Removes friction for casual viewers and kids, transforming bot-dominated comment sections into active human engagement and raising CTA clarity from 4.0 to 8.0+.
4. **Hard Mobile Safe-Zone Clamping in PIL Layout Engine** (visual, hook, effort low)
   - Clamp all interactive text, end-card containers, and subtitles above y=1470 in the PIL renderer, leaving the bottom 450px reserved for platform UI overlays. Shift subtitles into an anchored lower-third pill with 40% translucent night-blue backing and 15% reduced font size.
   - Effect: Resolves safe-zone layout violations (5.5) and ensures 100% subtitle legibility without clipping underneath Instagram/TikTok interface elements.
5. **Mandate Tactile Domestic Micro-Stakes and Sub-50s Runtime** (hook, story, effort low)
   - Refactor script prompt templates to ban abstract championship tropes ('secret playbook', 'find mate') in favor of tactile micro-stakes ('Can you help Vektor freeze the ticking oven in 2 moves?'). Restrict narrative setups to frames 1–3 (under 12s) and cap total reel duration at <= 50 seconds.
   - Effect: Replicates proven high-retention domestic themes, preventing kid drop-off caused by pacing drag and boosting completion rate.
6. **Automated High-Contrast 2D Cutaway Vector Board for Calculation Beat** (visual, effort medium)
   - Deprecate AI-generated 3D in-scene chessboards during the calculation and countdown beats. Replace them with programmatic 2D vector boards rendered via Python-Chess in night-blue and champagne-gold, paired with an expressive animated Vektor reaction close-up in the top corner.
   - Effect: Solves distorted piece perspective and visual clutter, ensuring instant mobile calculation legibility and sustaining retention past 7 seconds.
7. **Replace Rating Badges and Algebraic Jargon with Narrative Tactical Nicknames** (story, hook, effort low)
   - Strip technical FIDE rating tiers ('1600-1850') from visuals and captions. Translate dry notation (e.g., 'Qxh7+') into descriptive action phrases ('The Sneaky Rook Elevator', 'The Lightning Knight Fork') and add an explicit pause reminder during countdowns.
   - Effect: Removes calculation anxiety for young players and parents, keeping casual viewers immersed in the narrative without feeling intimidated.
8. **Streamlined Minimalist End-Card with Active Daily Streak Challenge** (visual, growth, effort low)
   - Clean up end-card clutter by stripping competing badges down to a single champagne-gold container between y=600 and y=1400. Replace passive follow copy with an active streak challenge: 'Day {streak_day} of training with Vektor. Follow to build your calculation streak 🔥'.
   - Effect: Focuses attention on a singular action, directly driving follow conversion and lifting likelihood_comment_share_follow from 5.0 to 7.5+.
