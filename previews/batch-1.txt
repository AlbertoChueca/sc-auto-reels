Masters don't calculate deeper 🧠
Masters don't calculate deeper. They calculate better.

🔬 The masters' first candidate was usually already the best move. The club players spent their time on moves that were never going to work. Depth was similar. Quality of the candidates was the whole difference.

✅ What to do: Before you calculate anything, list the forcing moves: every check, every capture, every threat. Then calculate only those.
