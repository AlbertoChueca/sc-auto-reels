# Playbook (updated 2026-10-12)

• Winning Hooks: Double down on mystery and big_dream. Unresolved enigmas and aspirational stakes stop the scroll and drive comments. Avoid funny_fail and generic challenge hooks; slapstick underperforms on chess shorts.
• Top Themes: Focus on personal blitz battles against Bishop Blaze and secret trials from Coach King. Completely avoid high-stakes tournament championship tropes—kids find formal tournament games sterile and distant compared to immediate rivalries.
• Narrative Pacing: Use story_length=normal. Viewers need emotional setup before the board appears. Use think_time=normal; fast timers suppress comments because kids lack time to calculate and type their answers.
• Puzzle Difficulty: Basic puzzles generate the highest interaction, while advanced puzzles work only when framed as Coach King's mystery tests.
• Calls to Action: Treat the viewer as Vektor's essential teammate. End with a direct prompt like: 'Blaze is grinning—what move saves Vektor?' Encourage commenting coordinates to keep Vektor's streak alive.
