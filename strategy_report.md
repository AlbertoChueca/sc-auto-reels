# Playbook (updated 2026-11-02)

• Format & Framing: Prioritize 'Tip' format over pure story. Anchor videos around catchy, concrete tactical rules (e.g., 'A pinned piece is not a defender', 'What does my opponent want?') that viewers can instantly apply.
• Winning Hooks: Double down on 'countdown_trap' and 'big_dream'. Hook within 1.5 seconds by showing an imminent tactical trap or a high-stakes goal. Avoid passive 'mystery' or 'funny_fail', which underperform.
• High-Performing Themes: Focus on 'cracking Bishop Blaze's secret playbook' and 'daily training to keep the streak alive'. Viewers connect with proactive prep, detective-like playbook cracking, and streak survival over low-stakes travel or homework rescue.
• Difficulty & Pacing: Keep tactics primarily at 'basic' difficulty with 'long' think time. Accessible puzzles give viewers confidence to pause, calculate, and comment their answer.
• Engagement Engine: Always hide the solution behind 'reveal=comments'. Explicitly ask viewers to rescue Vektor or verify his calculation to drive comments, saves, and shares.
